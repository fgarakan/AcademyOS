# AI Suggestions — Brian Demo Script

_Sprint 185 — Academy OS_

---

## Setup

- Sign in as a director with an active academy.
- Ensure at least a few active players exist in the system.
- Open the Director Dashboard at `/director`.

---

## Script

### Step 1 — Show the AI Suggestions card on the Dashboard

> "From the command center, you can see the Academy Intelligence section right alongside your regular alerts. This card shows you how many suggested actions are pending your review — nothing has changed yet, these are just recommendations."

Point to the AI Suggestions card in the middle panel.

---

### Step 2 — Click into AI Suggestions

Click **"Review suggestions →"** or navigate via the sidebar **Intelligence → AI Suggestions**.

> "This is the AI Suggestions review page — your Director approval desk. Every suggestion lives here until you decide what to do with it."

---

### Step 3 — Click "Generate Suggestions"

Click the **Generate Suggestions** button in the top right.

> "The system scans your current academy data — players without focus areas, private lesson requests, players due for reassessment — and creates a list of suggested actions. No data has changed yet."

Wait for the page to reload and show suggestions.

---

### Step 4 — Show the stat summary

> "Up here you can see a quick summary: how many are pending, high priority, accepted, and deferred. This gives you a sense of the backlog at a glance."

Point to the four stat cards.

---

### Step 5 — Open a suggestion card

Click the expand button on a suggestion.

> "Every suggestion has a title, a category, a priority level, and a confidence indicator. When I expand it, I can see exactly why the system is suggesting this."

Show:
- **Why this matters** section (the summary)
- **Evidence** — the data points behind the suggestion

---

### Step 6 — Show the impact preview

> "This section tells you exactly what would change if you accept it — and equally important, what will NOT change. The system is very explicit about its scope."

Point to:
- **If accepted** (lime-highlighted panel)
- **Will not change** (surface-raised panel with XCircle icons)
- **Recommended next step**

---

### Step 7 — Accept a suggestion

Click **Accept** on a safe suggestion (e.g., a player missing current focus).

> "When I accept this, it marks it reviewed and shows me the recommended next step — in this case, opening the player profile to set their current focus. The profile isn't changed automatically — I still have to go do it."

Show the "Accepted." result message.

---

### Step 8 — Deny a suggestion

Find another suggestion and click **Deny**.

> "Maybe this one doesn't apply right now — I can deny it with an optional note explaining why. It stays in the system for reference but leaves my pending queue."

Type a brief note, click **Confirm Deny**.

---

### Step 9 — Defer a suggestion

Find a third suggestion and click **Defer**.

> "Or I can defer it — kick it to a 'revisit later' list without dismissing it entirely."

Click **Confirm Defer**.

---

### Step 10 — Show the filter tabs

Click through **Accepted**, **Deferred**, **Denied** tabs.

> "Every decision is tracked. The director always has full visibility into what's been reviewed, what was accepted, and what was dismissed — and why."

---

## Closing message

> "The key thing to understand about Academy OS AI: the system learns and suggests. Brian stays in control.

> AI proposes. Director approves. System records.

> Nothing changes automatically. Nothing is sent to parents. No player levels move. Every suggestion is a recommendation — the director is always the decision-maker."
