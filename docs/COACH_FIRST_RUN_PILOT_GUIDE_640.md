# Coach First-Run Pilot Guide V1

**Sprint:** 640
**Date:** 2026-05-17
**Audience:** Coaches using DONNA wrap-up and the coach workspace for the first time
**Time to read:** 4 minutes

---

## What You Need to Know

Academy OS has two things for you as a coach:
1. **DONNA wrap-up** — a post-session conversation where you tell DONNA what happened. Takes 3–5 minutes.
2. **Coach workspace** — your view of sessions, players, and DONNA outputs at `/coach`.

Your director (Brian) reviews everything you submit before it has any effect. You're capturing — not changing anything directly.

---

## The Wrap-Up — Your Main Job

After every session, DONNA will ask you 7 questions:

| # | Question | What It Captures |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Who was here today? | Attendance, absences, unexpected arrivals |
| 2 | Did the session go as planned? | Session actuals vs. template |
| 3 | Any players who stood out? | Positive observations |
| 4 | Anyone who needs attention? | Concerns, regression, flags |
| 5 | Any follow-up for next session? | Reminders, drill adjustments |
| 6 | Anything a parent should know? | Parent-safe communication flags |
| 7 | Any notes just for you? | Private coach notes |

**All 7 questions can be skipped.** Say or type what you know — skip what you don't.

---

## How to Start a Wrap-Up

1. Log in as a coach at your Academy OS URL.
2. Go to `/coach` — you'll see today's sessions.
3. Click on a completed session.
4. Click "Start wrap-up".
5. DONNA opens the conversation.

### Using voice:
- Click the microphone button (Mic icon).
- Speak naturally — don't worry about exact phrasing.
- DONNA shows what she heard before accepting it.
- Click the checkmark to confirm or edit the text.
- Click the "X" to discard and re-speak.

### Using text:
- Type your answer in the text box.
- Press Enter or click Submit.

### Switching between voice and text:
- You can switch mid-session. "Type instead" link is always available.

---

## What Happens After You Submit

1. Your wrap-up goes to DONNA's structured format.
2. It enters the **director review queue** as a pending item.
3. Brian reviews it — he may approve, ask for clarification, or reject.
4. If approved, the relevant parts (observations, attendance flags, etc.) move to the appropriate systems.

**Nothing you submit auto-applies to player records.** Brian always reviews first.

---

## Common Situations

### "I forgot to mention something"
If you've already submitted, message Brian and he can handle it during review, or you can add a note at the start of the next session's wrap-up.

### "Voice isn't working"
Switch to text mode — click "Type instead" at the bottom of the wrap-up screen. Text mode works the same way.

### "I don't know the answer to a question"
Skip it. Every question is optional. It's better to skip than guess.

### "I flagged something for a parent — will they see it?"
No — not automatically. Brian reviews all parent flags first. Nothing reaches a parent without his approval.

### "I mentioned a player needs to move up a level"
DONNA notes it as a flag. Brian reviews it. A formal level-up goes through a separate assessment process — it doesn't happen from your wrap-up alone.

## Your First Wrap-Up — Walkthrough

1. After your first session, go to `/coach`.
2. Click on the session.
3. Click "Start wrap-up".
4. For each question, speak or type one or two sentences. Be honest and specific.
5. If DONNA asks a clarifying question, answer it or skip it.
6. After the last question, review the summary screen.
7. Click "Submit".
8. Done. Brian will review it.

**Tip:** Do your wrap-up while the session is fresh — within 30 minutes is ideal.

| "Tyler is struggling with footwork on wide balls" | Creates a needs-attention flag for Brian |

---

## Quick Tips

- **Be specific about players** — DONNA looks for player names in your answers.
- **You don't need to be formal** — speak the same way you'd tell Brian after the session.
- **Voice takes getting used to** — 2–3 sessions in, it feels natural.
- **Skipping is fine** — a 3-question wrap-up is better than no wrap-up.
