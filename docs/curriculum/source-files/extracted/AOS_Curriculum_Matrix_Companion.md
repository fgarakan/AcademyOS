# AOS Curriculum — Piece 1 Companion

**The 15 × 8 Matrix: How to Read, Use, and Extend the Spine**

---

## What this is

`AOS_Curriculum_Matrix.xlsx` is the developmental spine of the Angles Academy OS curriculum engine. 15 stages (Red 1 → HP 3) × 8 curriculum domains = 120 cells, each dense enough that a working coach can read and act on it. It is the foundation that every downstream piece (gates, drills, tactics, fitness, coach-facing translation) hangs off of. Get this right and the rest of the system is well-anchored. Get this wrong and downstream drift compounds.

---

## Design decisions

**Stage count: 15.** Five colors (Red, Orange, Green, Yellow, HP) × three sub-stages (.1 Foundation / .2 Intermediate / .3 Matchplay). This extends Tennis Australia's Hot Shots sub-stage logic — which they only apply through Yellow — across all ball colors plus High Performance. The result: 15 stages, 14 transition gates within the matrix, plus a 15th transition out of HP 3 into living-as-a-pro readiness.

**Domain count: 8.** Technical, Tactical (Court Mapping), Movement / Athletic, Competition, Mentality / Learning Behavior, Fitness Support, Assessment / Evidence, Level-Up Gates. These are the eight named in the prior research synthesis. Notably, the Tactical column is renamed *Tactical (Court Mapping)* to make the Angles vocabulary primary — middle / crosscourt / short angle / line / transition / endgame appears across the cells, not just as an abstract principle.

**Stage transitions are gated, not timed.** Every stage's Level-Up Gates cell specifies 3–4 observable, evidence-backed criteria. No criterion is "spent X weeks at this stage." The match-play criteria use win-rate windows (e.g., "≥50% over 12 weeks") rather than tournament counts — this matches Tennis Canada's WPDP framing of healthy ratios over time rather than checklists of events.

**Angles philosophy is embedded in every cell, not bolted on as a separate domain.** Specifically:

- *Learn / Train / Play* — assumed as the daily session structure throughout. The Technical, Tactical, and Competition cells together describe what each stage's Learn, Train, and Play blocks should contain.
- *Skill Track and Competition Track separation* — the Competition column inside this Skill Track matrix is the Skill-side touchpoint with the parallel Competition Track (Piece 6).
- *Court mapping vocabulary* — appears progressively from Red 3 (introduction of "middle") through HP 3 (full pattern library across surfaces).
- *Intention → action → skill* — Technical cells describe shots in the context of what the player is trying to *do* tactically at that stage, not as isolated mechanics.
- *Swinget as foundational warm-up* — appears from Red 1 onward, prefixed `[PROPOSED:]` because the exact integration cadence is academy IP that needs Farsh's correction.
- *Evidence-based level movement* — Level-Up Gates are written as observable events, not coach feelings.
- *Positive coaching language* (Doing Well / Working On / Current Focus / Next Step) — introduced at Red 1, reinforced at every stage.
- *Three diagnostic zones* (Behind / Between Legs / Green Zone ✓) — appear from Orange 2 onward as the Swing Check / Angle product becomes part of the system, prefixed `[PROPOSED:]`.

## The `[PROPOSED:]` convention

Any cell or sentence that starts with `[PROPOSED:]` is content this matrix is *inferring* about Angles academy IP rather than *reporting* on it. There are three main areas where this shows up:

1. **Swinget integration timing.** When does the Swinget enter daily training, how much volume, what's the swap point with the racquet?
2. **The Angle product use cadence.** When does The Angle™ become a weekly tool, when does it become a maintenance tool, when does it stop being central?
3. **Swing Check app cadence.** When is it diagnostic, when is it tracking, when is it just a periodic verification?

These need Farsh's review. Once corrected, the `[PROPOSED:]` prefix is removed and the cell becomes academy-locked content — meaning Pieces 2 through 10 can rely on it without re-asking.

For the build to keep moving, I will continue proposing Angles IP integrations across Pieces 2–10 with the same `[PROPOSED:]` flag, and Farsh can correct in batches.

## How to use this matrix

**As a coach reference.** Open to the Matrix sheet. Find your player's current stage in column A. The row across is their full curriculum picture for that stage. Frozen panes keep the stage column visible while scrolling.

**As a parent/director conversation tool.** The Stage Index sheet gives a one-line summary per stage. The Domain Index sheet defines each of the 8 domains. Together they make the stage progression explainable to non-coaches.

**As input to the next Piece.** Piece 2 (gate library) is built directly from column H (Level-Up Gates). Piece 4 (tactical decision-tree) is built from column D (Tactical). Piece 5 (drill library) is shaped by columns C, D, and E (Technical, Tactical, Movement). Piece 8 (coach-facing translation) translates each cell into Doing Well / Working On / Current Focus / Next Step phrasing.
