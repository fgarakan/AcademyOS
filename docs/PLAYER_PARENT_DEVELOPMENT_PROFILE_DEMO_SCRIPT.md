# Player + Parent Development Profile — Demo Script

Sprint 175 | 2026-05-02

Audience: Director, Head Coach, Academy Owner
Purpose: Show how Academy OS makes player development visible, structured, and safe across roles.

---

## Step 1 — Open Director Dashboard

Navigate to `/director`

> "This is the Academy OS Director Dashboard. From here you can see the state of every player, every session, and every development priority in one place."

---

## Step 2 — Click Active Players

Click the **Active Players** command card.

> "The Active Players drilldown shows every active player with their current level, current focus area, and development score. Each row links directly to their profile."

Point out:
- "Current Focus" label in each player row
- Score trend (green up / red down)
- One click to open the full profile

---

## Step 3 — Select / Open a Player

Click any player row to open `/director/players/[playerId]`

> "This is the player development profile. The Overview tab is the first thing you see — it gives you an immediate picture of where this player is."

---

## Step 4 — Show Development Summary (Overview tab)

Point to the **Development Profile Summary** card in the left column.

> "This is the Development Summary — the coach's structured picture of this player. It's the internal view. It shows:"

- **Doing Well** — what the player is already building on
- **Working On** — current development focus
- **Current Focus** — the priority area right now
- **Next Step** — the top active priority from the priority queue

> "Notice the 'Internal coach view' badge. This content never goes to parents or players automatically."

---

## Step 5 — Show Level Progress (Overview sidebar)

Point to the **Level Progress** card in the right sidebar.

> "This sidebar card shows where the player sits in the curriculum — current level, what level they're working toward, and whether they meet advancement criteria."

- Green CheckCircle2 = meets advancement criteria
- Clock = not yet eligible
- Lock icon = requires director approval to advance

---

## Step 6 — Switch to Notes tab

Click the **Notes** tab.

> "The Notes tab is where the coaching record lives. It starts with a Coach Snapshot."

Point to **Coach Snapshot** at the top.

> "The Coach Snapshot is designed to answer one question before a session: what should I pay attention to with this player today? It pulls the current focus, what they're doing well, what they're working on, their top priority, and the most recent coach note."

---

## Step 7 — Show Progress Evidence

Scroll down to **Progress Evidence** section.

> "Below the snapshot is the Progress Evidence timeline. Every coach observation is listed here with its visibility status — Internal or Coach note. The internal label means it stays inside the staff team. Nothing here flows to parents or players automatically."

---

## Step 8 — Show Parent-Safe Preview

Navigate to `/parent`

> "Now let's look at what parents see. This is the Parent Portal. Where the development summary would appear, we show a preview card."

Point to **Child's Progress** section.

> "The 'Preview only' badge makes it clear this hasn't been sent yet. The empty state says: 'Progress summaries will appear here after coach/director review.' This is the guardrail — no raw coach notes, no internal data. A parent-safe summary requires explicit review and approval before it goes live."

---

## Step 9 — Show Player Mission Preview

Navigate to `/player`

> "This is the Player Portal. The Today's Mission section uses mission-based language:"

Point to **Today's Mission** (empty state).

> "'Your next mission will appear after your coach reviews your progress.' Same guardrail — it's a preview. When a summary is approved and safe, the player can see: Your Strength, Your Mission, and their Next Win. Simple, mission-based language — not school report language."

---

## Step 10 — Explain the Guardrails

> "Here's what makes this safe:"
>
> - Raw coach notes stay internal by default
> - Parent-safe summaries are previews — they require review before publishing
> - Player-facing content is mission-based — no labels like 'weaknesses'
> - No level moves happen automatically — the director approves advancement
> - Every piece of content you just saw comes from the existing coaching record — no AI generation, no fake data
>
> "The result: the player development profile becomes the central proof of value for Academy OS. Coaches can see what matters before each session. Directors can see the development story for every player. And when it's ready — parents and players see a version that's been reviewed, approved, and framed the right way."
