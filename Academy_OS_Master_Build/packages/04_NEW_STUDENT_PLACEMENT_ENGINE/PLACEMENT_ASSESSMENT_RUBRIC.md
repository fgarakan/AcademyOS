# PLACEMENT ASSESSMENT RUBRIC
**Package:** 04 — New Student Placement Engine
**Version:** 1.0 | **Status:** Draft

---

## Overview

The placement assessment evaluates a new student across five dimensions.
Each dimension has a top-level score (0.0–10.0) and subcategory scores.

Scoring scale: **0.0–10.0 in 0.5 increments**

Overall score weighting:
- Technical: 30%
- Tactical: 20%
- Movement: 20%
- Competition: 15%
- Behavioral: 15%

---

## Dimension 1: Technical (weight 30%)

**What it measures:** Stroke mechanics and ball-striking ability.

| Subcategory | What to observe |
|---|---|
| `forehand` | Grip, swing path, contact point, consistency, spin |
| `backhand` | One-hand vs. two-hand, grip, contact, stability |
| `serve` | Toss consistency, pronation, power vs. direction |
| `return` | Positioning, reading spin, neutralizing fast serves |
| `volley` | Hand position, punch vs. block, movement to net |
| `overhead` | Tracking, footwork, execution under pressure |

**Score guide:**
| Score | Description |
|---|---|
| 1–2 | No technique established — beginner/recreational |
| 3–4 | Basic strokes present; significant mechanical errors |
| 5–6 | Technically functional; some consistency under neutral ball |
| 7–8 | Good mechanics across most strokes; reliable in rallies |
| 9–10 | Tour-level mechanics; consistent across all shot types |

---

## Dimension 2: Tactical (weight 20%)

**What it measures:** Pattern recognition, decision-making, and game style.

| Subcategory | What to observe |
|---|---|
| `patterns` | Use of opening patterns (e.g., cross → DTL, serve + 1) |
| `positioning` | Court position between shots, recovery position |
| `decision_making` | Shot selection under pressure, when to attack vs. defend |
| `game_style` | Baseliner vs. all-court vs. serve-and-volley tendencies |

**Score guide:**
| Score | Description |
|---|---|
| 1–2 | No tactical awareness — plays ball, not pattern |
| 3–4 | Begins to recognize basic patterns but execution inconsistent |
| 5–6 | Uses 2–3 reliable patterns; makes reasonable shot choices |
| 7–8 | Tactical awareness high; adapts to opponent style |
| 9–10 | Elite pattern play; constructs points deliberately |

---

## Dimension 3: Movement (weight 20%)

**What it measures:** Physical athleticism and court coverage.

| Subcategory | What to observe |
|---|---|
| `speed` | Reaction time, first step, max speed to wide balls |
| `agility` | Change of direction, split step timing |
| `recovery` | Court recovery position after wide shots |
| `court_coverage` | Range of court covered; defensive ability |

**Score guide:**
| Score | Description |
|---|---|
| 1–2 | Very limited mobility; struggles to reach moderate balls |
| 3–4 | Basic court coverage; slow recovery; limited agility |
| 5–6 | Adequate movement; functional for developmental groups |
| 7–8 | Fast, efficient; good split step; covers well |
| 9–10 | Elite movement; explosive, precise, excellent recovery |

---

## Dimension 4: Competition (weight 15%)

**What it measures:** Performance under pressure and match mindset.

| Subcategory | What to observe |
|---|---|
| `pressure_handling` | Does performance drop significantly under match pressure? |
| `consistency` | Ball-in-play rate; unforced error frequency |
| `match_tactics` | Does player adjust during a match, or repeat losing pattern? |
| `mental_resilience` | Recovery from missed shots; momentum handling |

**Score guide:**
| Score | Description |
|---|---|
| 1–2 | No match experience; avoids competition; breaks down immediately |
| 3–4 | Limited match play; consistency drops sharply under pressure |
| 5–6 | Plays competitively; maintains some composure; some adaptability |
| 7–8 | Solid match competitor; recovers from adversity; adjusts mid-match |
| 9–10 | Competitor mindset; consistent under high pressure; leads by example |

---

## Dimension 5: Behavioral (weight 15%)

**What it measures:** Attitude, trainability, and team fit.

| Subcategory | What to observe |
|---|---|
| `attitude` | Positive framing, growth mindset, willingness to improve |
| `effort` | Work rate, intensity during drills, energy in tough moments |
| `coachability` | Responds to feedback; adjusts quickly; asks good questions |
| `communication` | Communicates with coaches and peers; body language |

**Score guide:**
| Score | Description |
|---|---|
| 1–2 | Resistant to coaching; disengaged; behavioral concerns |
| 3–4 | Inconsistent effort or attitude; responds to coaching sometimes |
| 5–6 | Generally positive; learns from instruction; solid teammate |
| 7–8 | Eager to improve; high effort; excellent response to coaching |
| 9–10 | Leadership-level attitude; energizes others; role model |

## Assessment Format

### Duration
- Full assessment: 60–90 minutes on court
- Mini assessment (intake only): 30 minutes

### Structure
1. Warm-up rally observation (5 min) — movement, basic strokes
2. Structured feed drills (20 min) — controlled observation of each technical area
3. Point play / games (20 min) — tactical and competition observation
4. Behavioral observation throughout

### Tools
- Coach scores each subcategory 0.0–10.0 in 0.5 increments
- Text fields for strengths, weaknesses, priorities (required)
- Promotion ready flag (yes/no with notes)
- Overall written summary

---

## Scoring Notes

- **Do not round to round numbers.** Use 0.5 increments to capture nuance.
- **Score what you see, not what you expect.** Age or prior experience are context, not a floor.
- **Subcategory scores inform the overall** — the system calculates the overall automatically.
- **Strengths and weaknesses (text) are as important as numbers.** They drive the recommendation rationale.
- **Priorities (top 3)** become the player's initial development focus. Be specific: "forehand topspin consistency under pressure" not just "forehand."
