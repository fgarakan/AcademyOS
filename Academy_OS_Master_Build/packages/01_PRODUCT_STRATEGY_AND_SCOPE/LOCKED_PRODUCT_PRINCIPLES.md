# LOCKED PRODUCT PRINCIPLES

These principles are non-negotiable. They define what Academy OS is and is not.

## 1. Voice creates. UI confirms. Database structures. System executes.

Every feature decision maps to this four-word contract. If a feature doesn't support voice creation (even if text input is used in V1), it's wrong. If it bypasses human confirmation, it's wrong. If it doesn't store structured data, it's wrong.

## 2. Human approval is always the last step before execution.

AI recommends. Directors decide.
Voice proposes. Coaches confirm.
The system suggests. Humans activate.

This is not a technical limitation. It is the product philosophy. If coaches and directors don't trust the system, they won't use it. Trust requires control.

## 3. Structure first. Intelligence second.

You cannot build useful AI on top of unstructured data. Assessments must be scored, not written in free text. Sessions must have blocks, not just notes. Players must have levels and tracks, not just a name.

The depth of intelligence is directly proportional to the quality of the underlying structure.

## 4. The system must get smarter as it runs.

This is the moat. Day 1 the system knows what the coach told it. Day 365 it sees patterns no human can see at scale. The architecture must support this compounding.

## 5. Coach friction must be near zero.

Coaches will not fill in forms. They will not type paragraphs. They will talk. The system must make 30 seconds of voice worth more than 20 minutes of typing. Any feature that adds more than 5 minutes of coach work per session will not be used.

## 6. Parents need to feel the value, not just trust it.

Parents paying academy fees want proof of development, not promises. The system must produce specific, warm, professional communications that parents will read. If parents feel informed, retention goes up. If parents feel ignored, they cancel.

## 7. Every decision must be explainable.

A placement decision, a group move, a session intensity change — all must be traceable back to data and human approval. If a parent asks "why is my child in Group B?", the answer must be in the system. Audit logs are not bureaucracy. They are trust infrastructure.
